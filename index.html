Who We Are

A Foundation Built on Compassion & Service

Talk To Mutendwah Foundation is a registered non-profit organisation in Kenya, committed to promoting holistic mental health and well-being for adolescent girls, young mothers, and their caregivers through psycho-social support, digital literacy, menstrual health advocacy, and economic empowerment. We currently operate in Nairobi County and Kitui County (Ikutha).

Our Vision

A resilient society where young women, mothers, and caregivers are mentally whole, economically self-reliant, and equipped to thrive.

Our Mission

Holistic mental health through psycho-social support, menstrual health advocacy, digital literacy, sustainable agriculture, and economic empowerment.

Our Story

How It All Began — Faith Mbusi's Journey

Faith Mbusi founded the Mutendwah Foundation during COVID-19, initially delivering homegrown vegetables to children's homes in Athi River. Recognizing the children's deep emotional distress, she gathered fellow counselors to provide vital mental health support. This grew into intensive Facebook and YouTube live sessions, eventually leading to mental health broadcasts on KCB.

Driven by her own painful teenage experiences with unaddressed menstrual disorders, Faith launched Wellness in Flow — an outreach program educating young adolescent girls and breaking menstrual stigma. Her path shifted again when her baby was born with Down syndrome. Interacting with other caregivers revealed the unique, immense challenges of managing puberty for neurodivergent girls. In response, Faith established the Menstrual Summit to connect caregivers with experts on specialized products, nutrition, and mental health.

Passionate about education and women's empowerment, Faith also realized many young mothers are trapped by financial and childcare burdens, preventing them from attending college. To bridge this gap, she championed online, self-paced learning platforms paired with dedicated tutors, allowing young moms and their kids to learn flexibly, gain skills, and achieve financial independence.

Today, the Mutendwah Foundation stands as a holistic beacon of hope, seamlessly intersecting mental wellness, menstrual dignity, disability advocacy, and accessible education.

Faith Mbusi Founder, Director & Head of Mental Health

School Mentorship Project

Our School Mentorship Project guides students through the critical transitional years of high school by offering active, school-based mentorship modules focused on career development, practical life skills, self-esteem, and decision-making.

What we offer:
  • Structured career development sessions
  • Practical life skills training
  • Self-esteem & decision-making workshops
  • Mentor-mentee matching with professionals

Goal: Scale to more local educational institutions over the next 1–2 years.

Mama Angaza — Tech & Life Skills

Mama Angaza bridges the digital divide by equipping young mothers with globally recognized tech certifications through Cisco Networking Academy and Huawei.

Training tracks:
  • Cybersecurity essentials
  • Web design & development
  • Artificial Intelligence fundamentals
  • Digital literacy & life skills

Bonus: We sponsor brilliant, underprivileged students for advanced IT courses to boost their economic mobility.

Our biggest need: laptops, compatible devices, internet connectivity, mentorship, and scholarships.

Psycho-Social Support & Caregivers Summit

Our annual Caregivers Summit provides intensive mental health and health awareness platforms for 200 caregivers and adolescent girls (ages 13–25).

Focus areas:
  • Reducing caregiver burnout
  • Breaking neurodivergence stigmas
  • Psycho-social support resources
  • Discussing pad material discomfort

Long-term goal: Partner to donate non-plastic, eco-friendly pads to over 1,000 adolescent girls.

Agriculture & Economic Empowerment

We foster resilient entrepreneurship among young mothers through vocational training and climate-smart farming practices — with active operations in Kitui County (Ikutha).

Tracks:
  • Modern, climate-smart farming techniques
  • Soap making
  • Beadwork & artifacts
  • Crafts & handmade goods

Goal: Build a self-sustaining, community-led agricultural cooperative model for long-term food security.
